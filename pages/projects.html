Projects

Comic Ray Species Identification with Machine Learning

November, 2023 - Present
TALE extends cosmic ray detection down to energies of 10 PeV. I am studying whether reconstructed shower parameters can identify the primary cosmic ray in Monte Carlo data.

Trinity: High Energy Neutrino Detector

May, 2022 - October, 2023
The Trinity demonstrator searches for upward-going air showers created by earth-skimming ultra-high-energy neutrinos.

Ultra-High Energy Cosmic Ray Energy Spectrum using Hybrid Analysis with TAx4

August, 2017 - May, 2022
My thesis research used Telescope Array surface and fluorescence detector data to calculate the first TAx4 hybrid cosmic ray energy spectrum.

Surface Detector Monitoring

August, 2016 - May, 2022
I developed tools to compare daily surface detector errors with the access list and supported detector maintenance in the field.

sFLASH Measurement of Fluorescence Yield at SLAC

August, 2016 - May, 2018
The sFLASH experiment measured air fluorescence yield and its dependence on shower development at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory.
